Sleep In — three directions

Three ways to set the time. The top windows work, so try each one (scroll, drag, ↑ ↓, Return). The bottom row shows each one while a timer runs.

AStepper

Duration first. Nudge a big number up or down.

BClock

Sleep time first. Drag the moon to when your Mac should sleep.

CMenu bar timeline

No window. Drag along the next 12 hours, right from the menu bar.
